Money Market Fund Assets

Washington, DC; October 22, 2026—Total money market fund assets1 increased by $21.49 billion to $7.94 trillion for the week ended Wednesday, October 21, the Investment Company Institute reported today. Among taxable money market funds, government funds2 increased by $21.82 billion and prime funds increased by $3.23 billion. Tax-exempt money market funds decreased by $3.56 billion.
